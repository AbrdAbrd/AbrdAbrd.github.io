Building Our Permanent Home

The Chichester Jamia Centre Vision

Transforming a local building into a vibrant sanctuary of worship, education, unity, and community service.

Property Exterior

Historic Acquisition

Securing a landmark property in Chichester to establish the city's first dedicated Masjid. Providing parking, green space, and convenient access for all residents.

Spiritual Sanctuary

Main Prayer Hall

Spacious, climate-controlled prayer halls for Brothers and Sisters, equipped with modern wudu (ablution) facilities, sound systems, and wheelchair accessibility.

Education & Youth

Community & Madrasa Hub

Dedicated classrooms for Weekend Quranic School, youth mentoring programs, Islamic reference library, interfaith outreach center, and charity distribution drives.

Food Bank & Social Care

Serving vulnerable families across Chichester and West Sussex.

Financial Transparency

Where Your Donations Go (£580,000)

We operate with 100% transparency. Here is the exact budget allocation required for completing the project.

1. Building Property Acquisition £420,000 (72.4%)

Securing the freehold property contract in Chichester town area.

2. Refurbishment & Wudu Installation £95,000 (16.4%)

Converting commercial interior into prayer halls, installing wudu plumbing, carpets & acoustic insulation.

3. Planning Permission & Legal Fees £35,000 (6.0%)

Council change of use application, legal conveyancing, and survey reports.

4. Initial Reserve & Operational Contingency £30,000 (5.2%)

First-year insurance, utilities setup, safety compliance, and contingency fund.
